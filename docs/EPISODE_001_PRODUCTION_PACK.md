# MØNO — Episode 001 Production Pack

**Episode:** THE ARRIVAL  
**Runtime target:** 80–90 seconds  
**Master format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 24 fps  
**Visual identity:** A simple stylized 3D stick-figure alien inside photorealistic, monumental cinematic environments. The contrast is intentional and must remain consistent in every shot.

## Character lock — MØNO

Use the approved concept as the identity anchor:
- Smooth, pale, oversized round head.
- Large expressive dark eyes.
- Two thin curved antennae; left tip glows soft green, right tip glows soft pink.
- Very slim black stick-figure body, simple line-like arms and legs.
- Small dark travel cloak/scarf, flowing only when wind or motion justifies it.
- Small warm luminous circular chest emblem.
- No realistic human anatomy, no extra fingers, no costume redesign, no helmet, no facial hair.
- Keep head-to-body proportions, eye shape, antenna placement, colors and emblem consistent. Only pose, expression and camera angle may change.

**Rendering rule:** MØNO is stylized and clean, not photorealistic. Everything around MØNO should feel physically real: landscapes, atmosphere, water, rocks, clouds, buildings, reflections and lighting.

## Global visual language

- Photorealistic feature-film environments; physically plausible materials and atmospheric perspective.
- Wide cinematic compositions, strong scale contrast, deliberate negative space.
- Naturalistic volumetric light, restrained lens bloom, realistic shadows, subtle film grain.
- Palette: midnight blue and violet in space; cold cyan during arrival; warm amber at dawn.
- Camera movement should be slow, motivated and stable. Avoid random zooms and excessive camera shake.
- Keep the character readable against the environment; do not bury the silhouette in visual effects.
- Generate clean plates without text. Add titles and subtitles in the editor, not in image generation.
- Do not add extra alien characters or reveal the second light's source in Episode 001.

## Continuity prompt to prepend to every shot

"Use the exact same MØNO character identity as the approved reference: pale round oversized head, large expressive dark eyes, two curved antennae with a soft green left tip and soft pink right tip, very slim black stick-figure limbs, small dark travel cloak/scarf and a small warm glowing circular chest emblem. Preserve exact proportions and design. MØNO remains a simple stylized 3D character; the world and all environmental elements are photorealistic. Cinematic 16:9 feature-film frame, physically realistic lighting, no text, no logos, no watermark."

## Shot plan and generation prompts

### Shot 01 — The first signal (0:00–0:09)
**Narration (ORION):** "Before anyone knew its name, something was already on its way."

**Visual prompt:** Deep space, almost total darkness. A tiny distant point of light slowly pulses against a dense but natural star field. A faint wave of light travels through thin cosmic dust. The camera makes a very slow forward drift. Enormous scale, restrained mystery, no visible spacecraft yet. Photorealistic astrophysical environment, subtle highlights, deep blacks.

**Motion:** Slow push-in; the distant point pulses once or twice.  
**Sound:** Low atmospheric drone, one soft distant pulse.  
**Transition:** Match the pulse to the next shot's moving light.

### Shot 02 — The approach (0:09–0:19)
**Narration (ORION):** "It crossed a silence older than memory… toward a world it had never seen."

**Visual prompt:** A small mysterious luminous object glides silently above the curved blue edge of Earth at night. Vast cloud systems, faint city lights and a thin atmospheric glow establish scale. Keep the object distant and partly obscured; do not reveal its construction or occupant. Photorealistic orbital cinematography.

**Motion:** Camera tracks gently with the object; Earth slowly rotates below.  
**Sound:** Soft rising tonal texture, restrained sub-bass swell.  
**Continuity:** The light source remains small; no explosive entry or destruction.

### Shot 03 — First sight (0:19–0:29)
**Narration (ORION):** "And then, for the first time, the unknown had a place to stand."

**Visual prompt:** A breathtaking photorealistic alien valley at dawn: immense dark mountains, a distant ocean or river, low drifting mist, wind moving fine grass and dust, warm sunlight breaking through layered clouds. MØNO appears only as a tiny silhouette on a ridge, facing the horizon. The environment dominates the frame.

**Motion:** Begin very wide; slowly crane or dolly toward the ridge.  
**Sound:** Wind, delicate atmospheric harmonics, music begins to warm.  
**Continuity:** MØNO must remain small in frame until the reveal shot.

### Shot 04 — MØNO (0:29–0:40)
**Dialogue (MØNO / Miko):** "So… this is Earth?"  
**Narration (ORION):** "A world full of answers. And questions that have no names yet."

**Visual prompt:** Medium-wide cinematic reveal of the approved MØNO standing on a realistic rocky overlook. The simple stylized character is sharply readable against a vast photorealistic sunrise landscape. Its green and pink antenna tips glow subtly; the small chest emblem emits a restrained warm light. The dark travel scarf moves gently in the breeze. MØNO tilts its head with genuine curiosity.

**Motion:** Slow camera orbit of no more than a few degrees; subtle head tilt and eye movement.  
**Sound:** Pull music down under Miko's line; leave a small breath of silence after it.  
**Continuity:** This is the definitive first clear view of the character.

### Shot 05 — The humans (0:40–0:53)
**Dialogue (MØNO / Miko):** "They all look so different."  
**Narration (ORION):** "And yet, each carries an entire universe inside."

**Visual prompt:** Photorealistic modern city street at early morning, seen from MØNO's sheltered viewpoint. A few distant human figures walk naturally through warm pools of street light; reflections move across glass and wet pavement; vehicles pass softly in the background. MØNO is visible in the foreground edge, quietly observing. Do not make humans look threatening or exaggerated.

**Motion:** Gentle over-the-shoulder camera drift; natural background movement only.  
**Sound:** Subtle city ambience, distant traffic and footsteps beneath the score.  
**Continuity:** Human faces need not be identifiable; preserve a calm observational tone.

### Shot 06 — The connection (0:53–1:05)
**Dialogue (MØNO / Miko):** "Do you think they can see me?"  
**Narration (ORION):** "One of them already has."

**Visual prompt:** A quiet cinematic moment on the same street. One distant passerby pauses and turns toward MØNO. MØNO stands still; its large eyes widen slightly. The human makes a small, gentle acknowledging gesture. Naturalistic reflections, soft dawn light, shallow depth of field, restrained emotion. No crowd reaction.

**Motion:** Mostly still; a slow push-in toward MØNO's eyes, then a brief cut to the passerby's gesture.  
**Sound:** Music nearly disappears; one warm sustained note returns after ORION's line.  
**Continuity:** Keep the interaction subtle and believable.

### Shot 07 — The question (1:05–1:20)
**Dialogue (ORION):** "But Earth was not the only place watching."  
**Dialogue (MØNO / Miko):** "Wait… what was that?"

**Visual prompt:** Close-up of MØNO looking upward. Cut to a vast night sky above Earth. Far beyond the planet, a second tiny light appears for a moment, faint and ambiguous. The camera holds long enough for the audience to notice it, but never reveals its source. Return briefly to MØNO's curious, unsettled expression.

**Motion:** Slow tilt from MØNO to the sky; second light pulses once.  
**Sound:** A new distant pulse, then a clean cut to black.  
**Continuity:** Do not reveal a ship, creature or explanation. Preserve the mystery for Episode 002.

### End card (1:20–1:25)
**On-screen text (added in editor):** "MØNO" / "THE ARRIVAL — EPISODE 001"  
**Narration (ORION):** "The story has only just begun."

**Visual:** Pure black background, restrained white wordmark and one tiny green/pink signal pulse.  
**Sound:** Short original signature tone, followed by silence.

## Voice direction

### ORION
Deep, warm, calm, assured, subtly mysterious, emotionally restrained. Speak slowly enough to leave room for the images; avoid trailer-announcer exaggeration.

### MIKO / MØNO
Young-sounding, curious, clear, lightly delicate and energetic. Natural conversational delivery; wonder rather than childish overacting. Keep the same voice identity across episodes.

## Edit and mix requirements

- Dialogue must remain intelligible; duck music under every spoken line.
- Use 24 fps timeline and consistent motion cadence.
- Prefer motivated cuts, gentle dissolves and match cuts; avoid transition presets that call attention to themselves.
- Build ambience separately for space, upper atmosphere, dawn valley and city.
- Add subtitles only after dialogue timing is locked.
- End with at least 0.5 seconds of clean silence after the final signature tone.
- Do not upscale a weak or inconsistent character render and call it final; regenerate until MØNO's design matches the reference.
