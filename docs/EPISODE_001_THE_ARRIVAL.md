# MØNO — Episode 001: THE ARRIVAL

**Format:** 16:9 cinematic short  
**Target runtime:** 75–90 seconds  
**Language:** English  
**Tone:** mysterious, curious, emotionally restrained; original science-fiction storytelling  
**Narrator:** ORION — deep, warm, calm, confident, slightly mysterious; unseen and deliberately undefined  
**MØNO voice:** Miko — youthful, curious, lightly delicate, energetic, playful, natural and clear

## Creative direction
This is the first encounter, not an explanation of the whole universe. Let images, silence and sound carry the mystery. Avoid exposition dumps, comedy overload, or a fully explained origin. MØNO should feel unfamiliar but approachable. End on a question that makes the next episode feel necessary.

## Script and storyboard

### Scene 01 — A signal (0:00–0:09)
**Visual:** Deep black. A tiny point of light appears, then a quiet pulse spreads through a field of stars. Slow forward camera movement. No title card yet.  
**ORION:** “Before anyone knew its name, something was already on its way.”  
**Sound:** Low atmospheric drone, one distant tonal pulse.

### Scene 02 — The approach (0:09–0:19)
**Visual:** A small luminous object crosses a vast night sky above the curve of Earth. Clouds catch a faint reflection. Keep the object mysterious; do not reveal its full design.  
**ORION:** “It crossed a silence older than memory… toward a world it had never seen.”  
**Sound:** Soft rising texture, restrained low-frequency impact.

### Scene 03 — First sight (0:19–0:29)
**Visual:** Dawn over an unfamiliar landscape. The object settles out of frame. Wind moves grass; dust catches the first light. A small silhouette looks toward a distant city.  
**ORION:** “And then, for the first time, the unknown had a place to stand.”  
**Sound:** Drone opens into a delicate, warm harmonic.

### Scene 04 — MØNO (0:29–0:40)
**Visual:** Reveal MØNO in a clean, memorable silhouette. Subtle fabric/particle movement, expressive eyes, curious posture. Avoid over-designing the character.  
**MØNO / Miko:** “So… this is Earth?”  
**ORION:** “A world full of answers. And questions that have no names yet.”  
**Sound:** Music briefly softens to make the character line intimate.

### Scene 05 — The humans (0:40–0:53)
**Visual:** Through MØNO’s point of view: distant human silhouettes moving through a street or station. Reflections in glass, passing headlights, everyday gestures. MØNO watches without approaching.  
**MØNO / Miko:** “They all look so different.”  
**ORION:** “And yet, each carries an entire universe inside.”  
**Sound:** Gentle city ambience under the score.

### Scene 06 — The connection (0:53–1:05)
**Visual:** One person notices MØNO. A quiet pause. No fear montage, no exaggerated reaction. A small gesture of recognition passes between them.  
**MØNO / Miko:** “Do you think they can see me?”  
**ORION:** “One of them already has.”  
**Sound:** Score drops almost to silence, then one warm note.

### Scene 07 — The question (1:05–1:20)
**Visual:** Close-up on MØNO looking toward the viewer, then a cut to the night sky. A faint second light appears far beyond Earth.  
**ORION:** “But Earth was not the only place watching.”  
**MØNO / Miko:** “Wait… what was that?”  
**Sound:** A new distant pulse, then a clean cut to black.

### End card (1:20–1:25)
**Visual:** Minimal MØNO wordmark on black.  
**On-screen text:** “THE ARRIVAL — EPISODE 001”  
**ORION:** “The story has only just begun.”  
**Sound:** Short signature tone; leave a beat of silence.

## Editing and sound rules
- Keep the narration intelligible; music should sit below dialogue and duck during spoken lines.
- Use hard cuts only when motivated; otherwise prefer slow dissolves or gentle camera movement.
- Build a consistent palette: deep midnight blue, muted violet, dawn amber, and restrained cyan highlights.
- Add subtitles only after the final voice timing is locked.
- Do not generate final shots until the MØNO reference design is approved; character consistency matters more than visual quantity.
- Keep the ending's second light unexplained. That is the hook for Episode 002.
